## The Week as a Whole

The first week of September 2025 in Beijing opened warm and mostly dry, with the first four days posting highs in the low 30s °C under partly cloudy to overcast skies. The warmest days were **September 1 and September 4**, which tied for the highest maximum at **31.5 °C** (by average temperature, September 3 was the warmest day overall at 26.7 °C). The coolest night of the week was **September 6**, which dipped to a low of **18.4 °C**. The wettest day was **September 5**, which recorded **4.4 mm** of precipitation under light rain — it was also the coolest daytime of the week, with a high of just 24.7 °C. After that brief rainy interlude, conditions turned sunny on September 6 and ended the week cloudy and dry on September 7.
